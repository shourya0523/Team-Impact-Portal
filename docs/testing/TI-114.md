### 2. Rookie-card layout (Signing Day mockup, `apps/mockups/src/shared/ui.tsx` `ImpactCard`)

- Team colour stripe across the top edge of both faces (navy when the team has no colour; change
  the team colour as staff in the portal to check it follows).
- Big jersey number over the photo's top-left, logo top-right.
- Under the photo: name, then `Position · Team · College`, then a row of CLASS / MAJOR / DIVISION.
- Back: experience rows (up to 3), skills, about ("Looking for: …"), facts, contact only where
  shared with the viewer (never for teens, never in exports).

### 3. Motion: deal-in, tilt, shine, spring back

Web/portal (mouse and touch emulation) and iOS/Android (finger):

- Opening the card screen: the card deals in from below, slightly turned, settling in ~600 ms.
- Moving the pointer over the card (web) or holding a finger on it (app): it tilts toward the point,
  never more than 9° (corners are the maximum), with a light hotspot and holo band following.
- Release / pointer leaves: springs back to flat with a small overshoot; the shine fades out.
- Swipe sideways: the tilt lets go and the card flips as before; tap/click/Enter/Space still flip.
- Vertical scroll over the card still scrolls the page (app and touch web).
- The back face's shine follows the finger on the correct side (mirrored).

### 3b. Gyro tilt (phones)

iOS and Android app, and mobile Safari/Chrome on the portal (`gyro` prop, default on):

- Hold the phone still at any angle: after a few seconds the card is flat, whatever the angle.
- Tip the phone left/right and toward/away: the card tilts with it, up to 9° at about 20° of phone
  movement, and the shine slides across. Hold the new angle and it eases back to flat.
- Check the direction feels natural (tipping the right edge down leans the card the same way);
  the signs live in `tiltFromAttitude` if it feels inverted on a device.
- Finger on the card while moving the phone: both add up, still capped at 9°.
- Mobile Safari: the first tap on the card asks for motion access; deny it and the card still
  tilts by touch. Desktop browsers never ask and never tilt by orientation.
- Reduce Motion on: no gyro tilt at all (the sensor isn't started).

### 4. Reduce Motion

Turn on iOS Settings → Accessibility → Motion → Reduce Motion; Android Settings → Accessibility →
Remove animations; macOS/Windows "reduce motion", or DevTools → Rendering → `prefers-reduced-motion:
reduce`. Reopen the screen (the app reads the setting at start; restart it on native).

- The card fades in without moving; no tilt on hover/touch or from moving the phone; no shine at
  all; flips are instant.
- Turning it on while the page is open (web): the card drops back to flat with no shine.

## Known differences and limits

- Web PNG is drawn on a canvas, so it mirrors the flat face's layout rather than snapshotting it.
  It shows a "TEAM IMPACT" wordmark instead of the crest image, uses the navy palette (not photo
  colours), and a photo served without CORS falls back to initials.
- The in-app card, the export and the portal all build the rookie front from the same fields
  (`rookieFields` in `apps/athlete/src/components/cardData.ts`, and the portal's `athleteCard.ts`).
- Name fitting estimates glyph widths (Barlow Condensed); native adds `adjustsFontSizeToFit` and
  the web an ellipsis as a safety net if a real font runs wider.
