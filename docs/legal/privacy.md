This policy explains what we collect, who sees it, who helps us run the service, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. It is part of one agreement with our [Terms of Use](terms.md) and [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use.md), and shares their version number.

## The short version

- You must be 13 or older to have an account. Children under 13 never sign in; a parent runs their profile, and only their team and family can see it.
- **We don't collect health or diagnosis information about anyone.**
- You choose which contact details families see and, separately, which recruiters see. Recruiters only see athletes who have a published card and have turned on open-to-recruiting.
- Being findable through an identity community is a separate choice you can withdraw at any time, and every such search is logged.
- Every post, comment and event is checked by automated moderation before it appears.
- Delete your account and it is hidden at once and erased after 30 days.
- We don't sell your information, show ads, or use outside analytics companies.

## 1. What we collect

**Account details.** Name, email, a password (stored only as a one-way hash), your role, and your birth date. We use your birth date only to check your age (13 or older; 13 to 17 means a teen account) and to know when a teen becomes an adult. It is never shown to anyone.

**Profile and Impact Card.** What you add: photo, college, team, position, major, graduation year, hometown, experience, interests, and for alumni where you work and whether you're mentoring or hiring.

**Contact details.** Phone number, email and LinkedIn, and your choices about who sees each one.

**Children's profiles.** For a child under 13, the parent gives a first name, last initial, team, photo, age and an optional interests bio. We check the child's birth date when the parent adds them, then keep only their age. Children have no account, email or login.

**What you do in the app.** Teams and communities you join, posts, comments, events, RSVPs, reactions, reports and blocks.

**Your choices and agreements.** Each time you accept the terms (with the version you accepted), confirm as a teen that a parent or guardian agreed, change who sees your contact details, turn open-to-recruiting on or off, or opt in or out of identity search, we store a dated record.

**Activity log.** We keep a log of important actions in our own database, for safety, auditing and simple pilot statistics. For example: sign-ups, posts, a recruiter's searches, which athletes were shown in a recruiter's results, and when a recruiter viewed an athlete's card or contact details. Entries are kept for 365 days and then deleted. We don't use an outside analytics company.

**Devices.** If you allow notifications, a push token for your phone, so we can send you notifications. It is removed when you sign out on that phone.

### What we don't collect

- **No health or diagnosis information.** The only trace is that you are a member of a medical community you chose to join (section 5).
- No race, ethnicity or gender fields.
- No location tracking, no advertising identifiers.
- From resumes, no home addresses or birth dates (section 7).

## 3. Identity search

Recruiters can filter athletes by an **official** Team IMPACT interest community (for example, a first-generation college students community). This is called identity search.

- Joining a community and being findable through it are **two separate choices**. You are only included if you opt in for that community, and you can withdraw at any time in the app. Withdrawing takes effect for new searches straight away.
- Only official interest communities can be used as filters. Team, medical and private communities never are.
- **Every identity search is logged** with the recruiter, their company and the time, and Team IMPACT reviews the log.
- The whole feature sits behind one switch. Team IMPACT can turn it off at any time; the rest of search keeps working.

- A parent or guardian runs their child's profile and can change or delete it.
- Teens confirm at sign-up that a parent or guardian agreed, and we store that confirmation with the terms version. A teen's profile is visible only to their team and family, and teens never see athletes' contact details.

## 5. Medical communities

Medical communities let families and athletes support each other. Being a member is the only health-related trace in Team IMPACT, and we treat it carefully:

- Membership is never shown on your profile, never searchable and never a recruiter filter.
- Teens and recruiters can't join medical communities.
- Posts in them go through the same moderation as everywhere else, and members see them in their home feed.
- Team IMPACT can hide the whole medical community feature with one switch.

Please don't post diagnoses or medical details you wouldn't want other members to read.

## 6. Moderation and safety

Before any post, comment or event appears, its text is sent to an automated moderation service (OpenAI's moderation API) to check for harmful content. Some items are then held for a person at Team IMPACT to review. When you report something or block someone, we keep a record so we can act on it.

## 7. Resumes

If you upload a resume to fill your Impact Card:

- We read it automatically. Anything read from it is treated as data, checked against fixed fields, and **shown to no one until you review and confirm it**.
- Home addresses and birth dates are removed and never kept.
- The original file is deleted after it has been read, unless you ask us to keep it.
- When Team IMPACT turns the option on, the resume text (with your contact details removed) may be sent to a hosted AI service (OpenAI) to help read the fields.
