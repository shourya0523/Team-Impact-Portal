## 1. Who we are

Team IMPACT ("we", "us") connects children facing serious illness with college sports teams. The app keeps those teams, the children's families, Team IMPACT staff, alumni and recruiters from partner companies connected. The portal is the website recruiters and Team IMPACT staff use.

The legal entity that runs the service is [Team IMPACT legal entity name and address].

## 2. Who can use Team IMPACT

**You must be 13 or older to have an account.**

- **Children under 13 never get an account and never sign in.** A parent or guardian can add their child to their team. The parent runs the child's profile (first name, team, photo, age and an optional short bio about their interests) and is responsible for it. A child's profile is visible only to their own team and family.
- **Teens (13 to 17).** See section 3.
- **Coaches** are added by Team IMPACT staff or by another coach on the same team.
- **Athletes, parents and teens** join a team when a coach approves their request or when they scan the team's QR code from a coach.
- **Recruiters** sign up with a work email at a company Team IMPACT has onboarded as a partner. They get access after verifying that email address, and Team IMPACT staff may review each sign-up first. Personal email addresses can't be used.
- **Alumni** can sign up on their own or from a Team IMPACT invite.

You may not use Team IMPACT if we have suspended or removed you before, or if the law where you live doesn't allow it.

## 3. Teens: a parent or guardian must agree

If you are 13 to 17 years old:

- **When you sign up you confirm that a parent or legal guardian has read these terms, the Privacy Policy and the Acceptable Use Policy, and agreed to you joining Team IMPACT.** We record that confirmation, with the version of the terms, alongside your account.
- Do not sign up if a parent or guardian has not agreed.
- Your profile is visible only to your own team and family. It never appears in search, to recruiters, in card exports or in any community outside your team.
- You never see athletes' contact details.
- You can read and comment in communities you have joined, but you can post only to your team's community. You can't join medical communities.

Parents and guardians: by agreeing, you accept these terms on your teen's behalf and are responsible for their use of Team IMPACT. If you did not agree, or you change your mind, contact us at [privacy contact email] and we will delete the account.

- Give accurate information, and use your own name. One account per person.
- Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens on your account. Tell us at [support contact email] if you think someone else is using it.
- Your birth date is used to check your age and to know when a teen becomes an adult. It is never shown to anyone.

## 5. What each kind of member can see and do

What you see depends on your role and your team. In short:

| Member    | Can                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Athlete   | Build an Impact Card (your profile), post, comment, create events, choose which contact details families and recruiters see, turn open-to-recruiting on or off, join and create communities.                   |
| Parent    | See your team's roster and the contact details athletes chose to share with families, run your child's profile, comment, RSVP, join and create communities. Parents can't post or create events.               |
| Teen      | See section 3.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Coach     | Approve and remove team members, add coaches, manage team QR codes, post and create events.                                                                                                                    |
| Recruiter | Search athletes who have a published card and have turned on open-to-recruiting, see the contact details they chose to share with recruiters, and keep lists and notes shared with colleagues at your company. |
| Alumni    | Post, comment, create events, join and create communities, and flag yourself as mentoring or hiring so athletes can find you.                                                                                  |
| Staff     | Run teams, companies, moderation and the Team IMPACT community.                                                                                                                                                |

There is no in-app messaging. People reach each other through contact details the owner chose to share.

## 6. Your content

- You own what you post: posts, comments, events, photos, your Impact Card and your profile.
- You give Team IMPACT a licence to host, store, copy and show your content to the people you share it with in the app and portal, and to moderate it. The licence ends when your content is deleted, except for copies we must keep by law or that sit in backups until they expire (see the Privacy Policy).
- **Every post, comment and event is checked by automated moderation before anyone else can see it.** Some content is held for a person at Team IMPACT to review. We may refuse, hide or remove content that breaks the Acceptable Use Policy.
- Don't post anything you don't have the right to share, including photos of other people who haven't agreed.
- Post photos of a child, or tag a child, only in that child's team community. [Counsel question: whether such posts may ever leave the team. Until counsel answers, this rule applies.]

## 7. Contact details

Athletes choose which of their phone number, email and LinkedIn families on their team can see, and separately which recruiters can see. Recruiters see an athlete only while the athlete has a published card and open-to-recruiting turned on.

If you can see someone's contact details, use them only for the reason they were shared with you (team life for families, recruiting for recruiters) and follow the Acceptable Use Policy. What happens between people outside the app is between them; Team IMPACT isn't a party to it.

## 8. Extra terms for recruiters

- Use Team IMPACT only to recruit for your own company, and only while you work there.
- Don't copy, export, sell or share athletes' details outside your company, and don't contact an athlete who asks you to stop.
- **Identity search** (filtering by an official Team IMPACT community) shows only athletes who chose to be searchable that way. Every such search is logged with your name, company and the time. It may be turned off at any time. Never use it, or anything else in Team IMPACT, to discriminate against anyone.
- Team IMPACT can suspend you or your whole company at any time.

## 9. Impact Cards and resumes

You can fill your card by uploading a resume. We read the fields from it automatically, but nothing read from your resume is shown to anyone until you check and confirm it. You are responsible for what your card says. See the Privacy Policy for how resumes are handled.

## 10. Communities, including medical communities

Communities can be open or private. Private communities show their posts, events and members only to members, and their creator approves who joins.

Medical communities are for support between people with similar experiences. They are not medical advice and are not run by medical professionals. Team IMPACT does not collect health or diagnosis information. Membership of a medical community is private: it never appears on your profile, in search or to recruiters. Teens and recruiters can't join them. Team IMPACT may switch the whole medical community feature off. [Counsel question, see the cover note.]

## 11. Safety: reporting, blocking, suspension

- You can report any post, comment, event, community or profile, and you can block anyone. A blocked person can't see or reply to your content.
- Team IMPACT reviews reports and may warn, remove content, suspend or remove accounts, or suspend a recruiter's company.
- We may act without notice to protect children, members or the service.

## 12. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app. It is hidden from everyone straight away and its data is erased after 30 days, including from backups once their retention period has passed. Details are in the Privacy Policy. A parent deleting their account also removes the profiles of children they run.
