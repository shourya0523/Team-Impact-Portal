# Team IMPACT Acceptable Use Policy

> **Version:** 2026-11-draft.1
> **Status:** DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE, pending Team Impact counsel. Not in force until counsel approves it and the version is marked final.
> **Applies to:** everyone using the Team IMPACT app or portal.

Team IMPACT is a community built around children facing serious illness and the teams that draft them. These rules keep it safe. They are part of one agreement with our [Terms of Use](terms.md) and [Privacy Policy](privacy.md), and share their version number.

## Be kind: no harassment

Don't:

- bully, threaten, intimidate, shame or stalk anyone;
- post hate speech or attack people for who they are, including their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or illness;
- post sexual content, or anything sexual involving a minor (we report that to the authorities);
- post violent, graphic or self-harm content, or encourage anyone to hurt themselves;
- keep contacting someone who has asked you to stop, or who has blocked you, in the app or outside it.

## Use contact details only for what they were shared for

Athletes choose which contact details families and recruiters see. If you can see someone's details:

- **Families** may use athletes' details for team life: games, events and staying in touch with the team.
- **Recruiters** may use athletes' details only to talk about real opportunities at their own company.
- **Nobody** may copy contact details in bulk, add them to mailing or marketing lists, sell or share them, or use them to sell anything.
- Don't use what you learn in Team IMPACT to find someone's home, school or workplace without their agreement.
- Stop when someone asks you to.

## Be yourself: no impersonation

Don't:

- pretend to be someone else, or another organisation;
- sign up as a coach, recruiter, parent or alumnus when you aren't one, or use someone else's work email;
- make a profile for someone else (a parent running their own child's profile is the one exception);
- misrepresent your relationship with a child, a team or Team IMPACT.

## Protect children

- Post photos of a child, or tag a child, only in that child's team community.
- Never ask a child or teen for their contact details, to meet up, or to move to another app.
- Don't share anything about a child outside the app without their parent's agreement.

## Keep health information private

Medical communities are for support. Don't share someone else's diagnosis or medical details, and think carefully before sharing your own. Never use anyone's membership of a medical community against them.

## Recruiters

- Recruit only for your own company, and only while you work there.
- Never use identity search, or anything else, to exclude or treat anyone differently because of who they are. Every identity search is logged and reviewed.
- Don't scrape, export or automate access to the portal.

## Don't misuse the service

Don't:

- post spam, scams, ads or chain messages;
- post content you don't have the right to share, such as other people's photos without their agreement;
- try to get around moderation, blocks, suspensions or privacy settings;
- access accounts or data that aren't yours, probe or overload our systems, or use bots or scrapers;
- break the law.

## Report and block

- **Report** any post, comment, event, community or profile that breaks these rules, using the report option next to it. Reports are confidential: the person you report isn't told who reported them.
- **Block** anyone. They can no longer see or reply to your content.
- If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

## What happens if you break these rules

Every post, comment and event is checked by automated moderation before it appears, and Team IMPACT staff review reports. Depending on what happened we may remove content, warn you, suspend or remove your account, or suspend a recruiter's whole company. We may report illegal content to the authorities. If you think we got it wrong, contact [support contact email].
